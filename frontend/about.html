About Us

GTX Meals is a small, local meal-prep company - not a factory, not a chain. Every meal is made in small batches from fresh ingredients, prepared by our own in-house chefs rather than shipped in from some outside supplier.

We believe eating well shouldn't mean sacrificing flavor. That's why we focus on fresh, healthy ingredients in every meal we prep, so you can eat clean without giving up taste - made fresh, picked up fresh, and ready when you need it.
