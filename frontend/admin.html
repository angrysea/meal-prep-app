Manage menu

Ordering

Automatic: open Monday 11:00 AM to Friday 4:00 PM (Central). Customers are told Friday 2:00 PM - the extra two hours are a grace period. Force it open or closed for holidays or special weeks.

Ready date

Automatic: the Monday after ordering closes. It moves to the next Monday when ordering opens Monday 11:00 AM. For an unusual week (e.g. a Tuesday pickup after a holiday), set a different date - it only applies within 3 days of that week's Monday.

SMS reminders

Off by default. While off, weekly reminders due to a customer's texting preference are skipped (and logged) instead of sent.
