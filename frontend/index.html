How It Works

Ordering: Our weekly menu is updated every Monday by 11:00 AM. Orders placed during the week will be ready for pickup the following Monday.

Order Deadline: Friday at 2:00 PM or when all available spots are filled. Limited spots available each week!

Monday Pickup:

  • Georgetown: 8:00 AM–1:00 PM or 3:30 PM–6:30 PM at The Gym TX
    4112 Williams Drive #105, Georgetown TX 78628
  • Elgin: Pickup may be available upon request. Please email us to make arrangements before placing your order.

Freshly Prepared: All meals are prepared fresh the night before pickup, kept refrigerated, and ready to reheat whenever you're ready to enjoy them.

Payment: Payment is due when your order is placed unless prior arrangements have been made. Orders are not confirmed until payment is received.

Cash is preferred, but Venmo is also accepted (@lgraffeo88).

Questions or Other Payment Options? Email us at gtxmeals@gmail.com.

Thank you for supporting The Gym TX Meals!
